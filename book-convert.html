      Notes

      • Send to Kindle (email or app) accepts EPUB, PDF, DOCX, TXT, RTF and HTML — not MOBI or AZW3 any more. Convert those to EPUB here first.
      • EPUB → EPUB rebuilds the book from scratch (clean XHTML, complete manifest, language set). That fixes many books Send to Kindle rejects with an “E999” error.
      • PDF input extracts the text only: images, tables and page layout are dropped, and scanned PDFs (pictures of pages) have no text to extract. Kindle accepts PDFs as-is if you'd rather keep the layout.
      • DRM-protected books (most store-bought Kindle and Adobe books) can't be converted. KFX isn't supported.
      • MOBI, AZW3 and FB2 are read with foliate-js; DOCX with mammoth.js; PDF with pdf.js.
